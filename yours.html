Yours, 2025
Selected books, inserted images
on-going

Yours is an ongoing side project alongside the artist’s writing practice, in which she began composing an autobiographical fiction that interweaves themes of labor, queer desire, and her personal upbringing. As reading holds an equally significant role in her process, she takes notes and inserts images directly into books. Layering fiction with snapshots from her photo album and generating new meanings through the act of reading.

*Selected book list:
The Argonauts by Maggie Nelson
Bosses by Ghislaine Leung
Testo Junkie by Paul B. Preciado
All Fours by Miranda July
Paradise Rot by Jenny Hval
